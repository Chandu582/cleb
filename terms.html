TERMS OF SERVICE

Facility rules, equipment safety covenants, membership guidelines, and athlete decorum standards for training at Fire Fitness Elite in Chandi, Nalanda.

📅 Last Revised: January 2026 ⚡ Facility: Fire Fitness Elite Sanctuary 📍 Opposite Referral Hospital, Chandi
01

Membership Eligibility & VIP Pass Verification

Fire Fitness Elite maintains a dedicated performance environment tailored for serious athletes and dedicated fitness enthusiasts:

  • ✦ Age Requirement: Members must be at least 16 years of age. Athletes aged 16-17 require written parental or guardian consent before using heavy barbell equipment.
  • ✦ 10-Digit Mobile Verification: Every member profile is anchored to a valid 10-digit Indian mobile number for facility check-in and security records.
  • ✦ Complimentary VIP 1-Day Guest Pass: Valid strictly for first-time visitors who reside in Chandi, Nalanda, or nearby districts. VIP passes are non-transferable and limited to one per individual.
02

Membership Pricing, Subscriptions & Freeze Policy

Our membership tariff is 100% transparent with zero surprise maintenance charges:

  • ✦ Current Fee Schedule: 1 Month Membership is ₹500; 3 Months Transformation Tier is ₹1,400 (saves ₹100). All fees must be paid upfront prior to facility access.
  • ✦ Non-Refundable & Non-Transferable: Memberships cannot be transferred to another individual or redeemed for cash refunds after facility access has begun.
  • ✦ Medical Freeze Allowance: In the event of a documented medical injury, illness, or unavoidable examination travel, members holding 3-Month subscriptions may request a one-time membership freeze (up to 14 days) by notifying Coach Sonu Raj Patel in advance.
03

Sanctuary Rules, Equipment Safety & Etiquette

To maintain our state-of-the-art Olympic equipment and protect fellow athletes from injury, strict sanctuary etiquette is enforced:

  • ✦ Mandatory Weight Reracking: All weight plates, Olympic barbells, kettlebells, and dumbbells must be stripped and returned to their designated racks immediately after finishing each exercise. Leaving weights loaded on bars or benches is strictly prohibited.
  • ✦ Mandatory Barbell Collar Clamps: Safety collar spring clips or lock-jaw collars are compulsory on both sides of the barbell during heavy squats, bench presses, and military presses.
  • ✦ Clean Indoor Footwear Only: Athletes must wear clean, enclosed training sneakers. Street footwear carrying mud, gravel, or dust from the road is not permitted on the specialized rubber impact flooring. Barefoot or slipper training is strictly disallowed for safety reasons.
  • ✦ Hygiene & Sweat Towel: Athletes are required to bring a workout towel to wipe down benches, seat pads, and cardio machines after use.
Respect the Iron: Controlled descents are expected. While heavy deadlifts on rubber platforms are encouraged, deliberately slamming dumbbells or dropping loaded barbells from shoulder height onto pins damages equipment and will result in suspension.
04

Health Preparedness & Physical Exertion Waiver

High-intensity resistance training, powerlifting, and cardiovascular conditioning involve natural physical exertion. By enrolling at Fire Fitness Elite:

  • ✦ You affirm that you are in sound physical health and do not have unmanaged cardiovascular conditions, spinal injuries, or medical contraindications preventing strenuous exercise.
  • ✦ You acknowledge that all training, lifting, and machine usage is undertaken voluntarily. Fire Fitness Elite and its training staff provide form instruction and technique guidance, but athletes are responsible for knowing their individual physical limits.
  • ✦ Always request a spotter from Coach Sonu Raj Patel or certified floor trainers when attempting maximum personal record (1RM) lifts.
05

Personal Belongings & Day Lockers

Day-use storage cubbies and lockers are provided complimentary during your training session:

  • ✦ Lockers are for temporary use during active workouts only. Overnight storage of personal gear is not permitted.
  • ✦ Athletes are strongly advised against bringing high-value jewelry, excessive cash, or expensive luxury devices to the facility. Fire Fitness Elite management accepts no liability for unattended lost or misplaced personal items.
06

Code of Decorum & Zero-Tolerance Policy

Fire Fitness Elite is a sanctuary of mutual discipline, motivation, and athletic focus. We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against:

  • ✦ Any form of harassment, intimidation, derogatory speech, or inappropriate conduct toward female athletes, fellow members, or gym staff.
  • ✦ Verbal abuse, aggressive confrontations, or deliberate destruction of gym property.
  • ✦ Possession, distribution, or consumption of banned substances, alcohol, or tobacco products inside the facility premises.
Enforcement: Violations will result in immediate termination of membership privileges without refund, and severe offenses will be reported directly to local authorities in Chandi, Nalanda.
