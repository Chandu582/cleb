PRIVACY PROTOCOL

How Fire Fitness Elite protects, encrypts, and handles athlete biometric telemetry, membership records, and physical training data at our facility in Chandi, Nalanda.

📅 Effective: January 2026 ⚡ Facility: Opposite Referral Hospital, Chandi 🛡️ Compliance: Indian IT Act & SPDI Rules
01

Biometric Telemetry & Vitals Tracking

At Fire Fitness Elite, we leverage modern fitness telemetry to help you optimize strength gains, cardiovascular conditioning, and metabolic expenditure. When you utilize our bio-telemetry pods, smart scales, or macro tools:

  • ✦ Physical Composition Metrics: Height, weight, calculated Body Mass Index (BMI), estimated body fat percentage, and Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) are processed solely for tailored workout recommendations.
  • ✦ Cardio & Heart Rate Data: Heart rate zones and estimated active calorie burns recorded during treadmill or HIIT velocity sessions are temporary session telemetry.
  • ✦ Zero Commercial Exploitation: Fire Fitness Elite strictly does not sell, license, or broker your personal biometric telemetry to third-party ad networks, pharmaceutical firms, or insurance brokers.
02

Athlete Registration & Mobile Communications

To issue your membership access, generate your 1-Day VIP Pass, or ensure facility security, we collect:

  • ✦ Athlete Name & Contact Details: Full legal name, official 10-digit Indian mobile number, and email address.
  • ✦ WhatsApp Telemetry & Alerts: We use your WhatsApp number exclusively for vital facility announcements, subscription expiry reminders, VIP pass verification codes, and direct trainer advisories from Coach Sonu Raj Patel.
  • ✦ Emergency Contact: For high-intensity powerlifting and heavy barbell training, an emergency contact number is securely stored on file.
Anti-Spam Guarantee: We despise spam as much as you do. You will never receive automated marketing calls or unsolicited third-party promotions through our member database.
03

Facility Video Telemetry & 24/7 CCTV

For the safety of all members, female athletes, staff, and expensive Olympic equipment, high-definition closed-circuit television (CCTV) cameras operate continuously throughout the facility:

  • ✦ Surveillance Zones: Cameras monitor the main barbell training floor, dumbbell racks, cardio deck, reception entrance, and exterior parking zones.
  • ✦ Absolute Privacy Zones: Restrooms, changing stalls, and shower facilities are 100% strictly camera-free zones under all circumstances.
  • ✦ Footage Retention: Security recordings are stored on encrypted local servers with automated 30-day overwrite cycles, accessible solely by authorized gym management or law enforcement authorities upon official request.
04

Financial Transaction Integrity

All membership transactions (₹500 for 1 Month, ₹1,400 for 3 Months) conducted via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or Cash at our Chandi facility are processed securely:

  • ✦ We do not store bank account PINs, credit/debit card numbers, or UPI credentials on our servers.
  • ✦ Payment receipts and transaction UTR numbers are archived purely for accounting reconciliation and membership validity verification.
05

Athlete Rights & Right to Expungement

As a registered athlete of Fire Fitness Elite, you maintain complete sovereignty over your recorded data:

  • ✦ Access & Review: Request a review of all physical telemetry metrics and membership logs associated with your phone number.
  • ✦ Right to Expunge: When you discontinue your membership, you may request the permanent deletion of your phone number, biometric telemetry, and profile logs from our active roster.
